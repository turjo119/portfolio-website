    I am an embedded software engineer with over 3 years of work experience in embedded development projects. As an embedded developer, I have extensive experience in C/C++, Python and FreeRTOS having developed applications on the Espressif ESP32, ARM Cortex M4 and STM32 Nucleo microcontroller development boards for IoT projects. I have also several years of expertise in electronic circuit design and simulation in Pspice as well as PCB design in KiCad/Altium Designer to design custom sensor suite for Industry 4.0 applications. I am currently working on projects in Zephyr to build cross-platform IoT applications. I see myself as a maker and my spare time work on DIY electronic projects which you can find on my YouTube.
